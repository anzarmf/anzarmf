## 🚀 Featured Project

### [NotchKu](https://github.com/anzarmf/notchku) — Interactive Notch for Windows

A small interactive notch at the top of your screen: music, calendar, todo, Pomodoro and weather — hover to open, `Alt+N` to toggle. Built from scratch to stay light, fast, and local-first.

![Rust](https://img.shields.io/badge/Rust-000000?style=flat-square&logo=rust&logoColor=white)
![Tauri](https://img.shields.io/badge/Tauri-24C8DB?style=flat-square&logo=tauri&logoColor=white)
![JavaScript](https://img.shields.io/badge/JavaScript-F7DF1E?style=flat-square&logo=javascript&logoColor=black)
![Windows](https://img.shields.io/badge/Windows-0078D6?style=flat-square&logo=windows&logoColor=white)

- 🎵 Music controls with progress & volume
- 📅 Calendar — month view + next meeting
- ✅ Todo, saved locally
- 🍅 Pomodoro 25/5 focus timer
- 🌤️ Weather via Open-Meteo, no API key needed

> Your data stays on your PC. No account, no tracking.

**[⬇️ Download NotchKu for Windows (.exe)](https://github.com/anzarmf/notchku/releases/latest/download/NotchKu_0.1.1_x64-setup.exe)** — free, no account needed.
